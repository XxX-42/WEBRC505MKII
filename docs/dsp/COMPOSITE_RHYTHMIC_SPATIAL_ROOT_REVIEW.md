# Composite, rhythmic and spatial processor checkpoint

Ten additional standalone effects are implemented: Radio (8), Sustainer (11), Slow Gear (13), Isolator (27), Stereo Enhance (31), Pattern Slicer (34), Step Slicer (35), Reverse Delay (38), Gate Reverb (48) and Reverse Reverb (49). Factory and host/Browser integration are separate ongoing work; this checkpoint commits actual processor implementations and functional evidence.

Root verified seven direct/transitive composite inputs and thirteen rhythmic/spatial inputs against their archived before/after manifests and current source bytes. Root independently reran all four archived Release executables successfully. Unfiltered compile commands, logs and copies are in `composite4-immutable-snapshot-20261009T155918Z` and `rhythmic-spatial-combined-review-20261009T155945Z` under `shared/dsp/benchmarks/results`. The compiler is MSVC 19.29.30159.0, toolset 14.29.30133. The supplemental root indexes hash source and evidence bytes observed at review time, including the rerun executables; the original producer manifests did not pin binary hashes.

The composite fixtures cover Radio drive automation (maximum audio step 0.0342999), Sustainer gain automation (0.0032944), Slow Gear high-frequency carrier stability and its smoothed onset/rearm (maximum normalized gain step 0.011322), stereo response, partition invariance and zero process allocations. These are individual fixtures rather than a comprehensive listening/quality gate.

Root found and required fixes for two defects before promotion. Changing rhythmic edge length during a transition originally produced gain jumps of 0.461139 and 0.994338. Edge length now makes a bounded logarithmic transition. The independent DC/440 Hz fixture reports excess automation gain-step above the ordinary static gate-edge slope of approximately 0.001881 and 0.0000592. The 0.1–10 ms contract rejects 20 ms. Existing spectral, clock, 64/128/256 partition, event and no-allocation fixtures also pass. Root found Gate Reverb incorrectly gating the dry subtraction; it now keeps the dry coefficient fixed and gates only the FDN wet return. The closed gate returns zero at Wet=1 and exact half-level dry at Wet=0.5.

Spatial tests retain impulse energy, first/peak frames, stereo energy and 64-event allocation/free counts. Reverse geometry rounds 0.100123/0.00517 seconds to 4806/248 frames; reverse staging is 9364 frames. The reused ReverseSegment intentionally forwards live audio before its first reverse segment and crossfades to reversed playback. Consequently Wet=1 Reverse Delay has an immediate forward impulse during startup, and Reverse Reverb has an immediate FDN return before the reversed tail. Staging is not first-output latency, measured group delay or end-to-end latency. FDN stereo diffusion from left-only excitation is intentional channel interaction; recording still retains two channels.

No event-burst deadline distribution or full product graph stress is qualified by these snapshots. The previous failed edge-automation and pre-fix spatial captures remain unchanged. Formal official parameter mapping, comprehensive quality, three pitch routes and the 30-minute integrated runtime gates remain incomplete.
